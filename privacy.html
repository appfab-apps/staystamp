Privacy policy

Last changed: 24 November 2026

In short

The Staystamp app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own.

What the app stores

The trips you enter (country, first day, last day) and whether you have seen the introduction are saved in one file in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. We cannot see it.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this file can be part of that normal device backup, like other app data. Apple’s terms apply to it.

Backup files

When you tap “Export backup”, the app creates a file with your trips and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where the file goes, and from then on it is in your control. When you tap “Restore backup”, the app reads only the file you pick.

While exporting or restoring, a copy of the file can remain in the app’s temporary storage on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete trips inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stores on your iPhone. Backup files you saved elsewhere, and device backups, have to be deleted where you keep them.

Outside the app

  • Links in Settings. “Privacy policy” and “Support” open this website in your browser. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address; see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Apple. Depending on your own device settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics about apps with their developers. If you send TestFlight feedback, Apple passes it on to us with the details shown by TestFlight.
  • GitHub Issues. If you contact us there, what you write is public. Please do not include personal, passport or travel details.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new text appears on this page with a new date. For questions, use the GitHub Issues page.
