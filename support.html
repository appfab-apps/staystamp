How are the 90/180 days counted?

You may stay no more than 90 days in any 180-day period. For each day of your stay, the app looks at that day and the 179 days before it and counts your Schengen days.

Your entry day and exit day both count as full days. A day counts once even if you were in two Schengen countries.

Which countries count?

  • The 29 Schengen countries, including Croatia from 1 January 2023, and Bulgaria and Romania from 31 March 2024, whatever border you crossed.
  • Monaco, San Marino and Vatican City count as Schengen. Andorra does not.
  • The Canary and Balearic Islands count as Spain; Madeira and the Azores count as Portugal. Åland counts as Finland.
  • Greenland, the Faroe Islands, Svalbard, the French and Dutch overseas territories and Gibraltar do not count.
  • Cyprus, Ireland and the United Kingdom do not count.

Why do yearly totals differ from the 90/180 count?

The Yearly screen counts every calendar day a trip covers for that trip’s country. A travel day counts for both countries, so the yearly totals can add up to more than the days of the year.

How do I back up my trips?

Open Settings (the round button at the top right) and tap “Export backup”. In the share sheet, choose “Save to Files” or any other place you trust.

To bring the trips back, for example on a new iPhone, tap “Restore backup” and pick that file. The trips in the file replace the ones on the phone; the app asks you first.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works offline and your trips stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Not official advice

Staystamp is not official advice. Residence permits, long-stay visas, bilateral agreements and other exceptions are not taken into account. Check with the authorities before you travel; the border officer always has the final say.
