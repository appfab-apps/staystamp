Know your Schengen days before you book.

Staystamp is an iPhone app that counts your days in the Schengen area (no more than 90 days in any 180) and the days you spend in each country every year.

What it does

  • Shows how many days you have used and how many are left today.
  • Shows when days come back.
  • Checks a planned trip, or tells you how long you can stay from a date you choose.
  • Adds up the days you spend in each country, year by year.
  • Saves your trips to a backup file you keep, and restores them from it.

Not official advice

Staystamp is not official advice and is not connected to the European Union or any government. Check with the authorities before you travel; the border officer always has the final say.

Your trips stay on your phone

There is no account and no server. Read the privacy policy, or get help on the support page.
